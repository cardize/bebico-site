Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: Bebico does not collect any personal data. There is no account, server, advertising or analytics. What you enter about your baby is stored only on your phone and never reaches us.

What does the app store?

The following information you enter is stored only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • Your baby's details: name, date of birth, sex and the vaccine schedule choice
  • Daily log: feedings (nursing, bottle, solids), sleep and diaper entries, and notes
  • Health records: vaccines, weight, length and head circumference measurements, doctor visits, doctor or clinic name, cost and notes
  • Your settings: reminder time, language and theme

None of this is sent to us or to third parties. When you delete the app, all of it is removed from the phone.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Their content is created on your phone and is not sent to any server. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's Settings app.

Backups

"Save backup" writes all your data to a single file on your phone and opens the share sheet. You decide where to save it (for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files); we do not receive this file and cannot see it. Those services store the file under their own privacy policies.

External links

The source link in Settings opens the vaccine schedule page of the Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Health in your browser. That page is covered by the Ministry's own privacy policy; the app does not send any information about you to it.

Children

Bebico is made for parents and other adults caring for a baby; it is not designed to be used by children. What you enter about your baby does not leave your phone, and the app does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the changes on this page with the date of the update.
