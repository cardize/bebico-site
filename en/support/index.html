Support

Having a problem or a suggestion? Write to msakaryawork@gmail.com; we usually reply within 2 business days.

I'm not getting reminder notifications

Check that notifications are allowed in your phone's Settings → Notifications → Bebico. In the app, pick the reminder time in Settings → Reminders. Reminders are set for doses in the vaccine schedule that are not done yet and for records with a "next dose" or "check-up" date.

Where does the vaccine schedule come from?

It is Türkiye's national childhood immunization schedule as updated by the Ministry of Health on 1 September 2026 (asi.saglik.gov.tr). Dose dates are calculated from your baby's date of birth and are for information only; follow the appointment your family doctor gives you. Bebico does not give medical advice.

I added my baby late and past vaccines show as overdue

When you add the baby, choose "All done" in the question that appears and the vaccines up to today are marked as done. To correct them later, open the baby page → Vaccine schedule → "Show the full schedule" and tap a dose: if it is not done it gets logged, if it is done the record opens (you can change the date or delete it).

I live outside Türkiye and the schedule doesn't fit

On the baby page tap edit (pencil) → Vaccine schedule → "Don't use". Add vaccines yourself with "Add vaccine"; if you enter a "next dose" date you get a reminder when it is due.

How do I end a sleep?

If you save a sleep with "Woke up" left empty, it counts as in progress and a "Woke up" button appears on the Today card; tap it when your baby wakes. To correct the time later, tap the sleep entry in the day's log.

How do I see only diapers (or feedings, or sleep)?

Tap the Feedings, Sleep or Diapers box on the Today card. The history of that type opens with a chart of the last 7 days and all entries day by day; switch between types at the top.

I want to add an entry to an earlier day

Pick the day in the strip on the Today card (use the arrows to go further back), then add the entry from "Quick add"; it opens with that day's date.

I'm switching phones. What happens to my data?

Your data is stored only on your phone. On the old phone, use Settings → "Save backup" to create a backup file and save it to iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the new phone, use Settings → "Restore from backup" and pick that file.

How do I change the language or theme?

In the app, go to Settings → Appearance and pick the language (Türkçe / English) and the theme (light / dark). "System" follows your phone's setting.

What happens if I delete a baby?

The baby and all of its records are deleted. This cannot be undone; save a backup first if you need to.
